WHICH FILE TO USE
svg/    Best for the website. 8-9 KB each, razor sharp at any size, transparent.
        Pauses automatically for visitors who turn on "reduce motion."
video/  .webm has a transparent background (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).
        .mp4 is on white, for Safari, social posts and page builders.
        Both are 1200x900.
gif/    600x450 on white. For email newsletters only.
